Printer Help FAQ

Quick answers.

Can you help with HP, Canon, Epson or Brother printers?

I can troubleshoot common consumer printer setup and connectivity issues across major brands. What can be fixed depends on the specific printer, device compatibility, software and hardware condition.

Can printer problems be handled remotely?

Some can. If the printer is powered on and reachable from the computer, software, driver, account and setting issues may be possible to handle remotely. Physical network or hardware issues may need an on-site visit.

Do you repair broken printer hardware?

No. I focus on technology setup, configuration and troubleshooting rather than internal printer hardware repair.
