Website Design FAQ

What small-business owners usually want to know.

Do you use a website builder?

I build custom websites when that is the best fit and can manage the technical side for clients who do not want to maintain code or hosting themselves. The exact build and editing setup depends on the project.

Can I keep my domain?

Yes. Your business should control its domain and critical business accounts. I can help organize the domain, DNS, hosting connection, and access.

Can you help my website rank in Google?

I build SEO foundations and can improve page structure, local service content, internal links, structured data, crawlability, and Google Business consistency. No legitimate provider can guarantee a specific Google ranking.

Can you redesign a website that already exists?

Yes. I can review the current site and determine whether improving it or rebuilding it is the better use of time and money.
