Local visibility

For Michigan service businesses, the website and Google presence should reinforce each other.

A local business needs consistent service language, location signals, contact information, useful customer-focused pages, and a Google Business Profile that accurately reflects the business.

A website alone does not guarantee rankings. Reviews, local citations, links, business prominence, competition, proximity, and ongoing content all affect visibility. We build the website foundation so those signals have somewhere strong to point.

Own the assets

Your business should control the domain, website, email, analytics, and major platform accounts.

Separate search intent

Give important services their own useful pages so Google and customers can understand what each page is about.

Measure what matters

Track calls, inquiries, bookings, search impressions, and the pages that actually generate business.

Website FAQ

What small-business owners usually want to know.

Do I own my domain and website?

We strongly prefer that the business owns and controls its critical accounts and assets. The exact ownership and service arrangement is documented for each project.

Can you improve an existing website instead of rebuilding it?

Yes, when the current platform and access make that practical. We can assess whether improvement or replacement is the better use of money.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No. Reputable SEO work cannot guarantee a particular organic ranking. We can improve the technical foundation, search targeting, content, local signals, and measurement so the business has a stronger chance to earn visibility.

Can the website connect to my business process?

Yes. Forms, email, CRM, scheduling, notifications, and workflow tools can often be connected so website inquiries have a clear next step. See workflow automation.
