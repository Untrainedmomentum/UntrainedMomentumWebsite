Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Untrained Momentum, LLC  ·  Big Rapids, Michigan

Who We Are

Untrained Momentum, LLC is a Michigan-based business providing digital presence, website design, and business support services to small businesses, startups, and nonprofits. Our website is located at www.untrainedmomentum.com. Questions about this policy can be directed to support@untrainedmomentum.com.

What Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out our inquiry form or contact us. This includes:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your city and state
  • Information about your business or project
  • Your responses to our intake questionnaire

We do not collect payment information directly. Any payments are processed through third-party payment processors and subject to their privacy policies.

We may also collect basic website usage data through analytics tools such as Google Analytics, including pages visited, time on site, and general location data. This data is aggregated and not tied to personally identifiable information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and determine whether we're a good fit
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Deliver services you have engaged us to provide
  • Follow up on referrals and reward eligible referrers
  • Improve our website and service offerings

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Inquiry form submissions are processed through Netlify Forms and stored securely. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to deliver services and fulfill our business obligations, or until you request deletion.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to understand how visitors use the site. These are small files stored on your device that help us improve the user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended if you do.

We use Google Analytics to understand general site traffic. Google's use of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in the operation of our website and business:

  • Netlify — form processing and website hosting
  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis
  • Cloudflare — DNS and content delivery

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We encourage you to review them directly.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us at support@untrainedmomentum.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact us to exercise any applicable rights.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
