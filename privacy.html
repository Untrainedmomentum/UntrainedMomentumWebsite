Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Untrained Momentum, LLC may receive, why we use it, and how we try to handle it responsibly.

Who we are

Untrained Momentum, LLC is a Michigan-based business providing small-business technology and operations support, websites, Microsoft 365 assistance, workflow and automation services, business startup support, and local home technology assistance.

Our website is untrainedmomentum.com .

Information you choose to give us

We may collect information you provide when you:

  • submit a contact form
  • send an email or message
  • call us
  • schedule a consultation
  • request technology or business support
  • become a customer
  • provide information necessary for us to complete agreed work

Depending on the situation, that information may include your name, email address, telephone number, general location, business information, service details, device information, account information, screenshots, files, or other information you voluntarily provide.

Information related to technology support

When providing computer, Microsoft 365, account, smart-home, family technology, or other technical assistance, we may temporarily see information stored on or displayed by your devices or accounts.

We try to limit access to what is reasonably necessary to perform the work you requested.

Whenever practical, customers should enter their own passwords, security codes, payment information, and other sensitive credentials.

We do not intentionally retain customer passwords or authentication codes unless retention is specifically necessary for an agreed service and the customer has authorized it.

Family technology and parental-control services

Some local technology services may involve helping a parent or legal guardian configure parental controls, family accounts, screen-time settings, location-sharing features, device-finding tools, smart-home notifications, or other family technology settings.

These services are performed at the direction of a parent, guardian, or other person authorized to manage the applicable account or device.

We do not provide covert monitoring services and do not intentionally collect information from children for marketing purposes.

Children

This website is directed to adults seeking business or technology services. It is not intended for children under 13 to submit personal information directly.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly through the website without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review the situation and delete the information when appropriate.

Information collected automatically

Our website, hosting provider, security systems, and other technology services may automatically receive basic technical information when someone visits the site.

This can include information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, timestamps, referring pages, and technical error or security information.

This information may be used for website operation, security, troubleshooting, performance, and general usage analysis.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • respond to questions and service requests
  • schedule consultations and appointments
  • provide agreed services
  • communicate about active work
  • prepare estimates, invoices, and records
  • improve our services and website
  • troubleshoot technical problems
  • protect our systems, customers, and business
  • maintain required business records
  • meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information

Untrained Momentum does not sell personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

Forms, scheduling, email, and other service providers

We use third-party services to help operate the business. These may include services for:

  • website hosting
  • contact forms
  • email
  • calendar scheduling
  • cloud storage
  • business productivity tools
  • payments and invoicing
  • analytics
  • security
  • other business functions

Information submitted through these services may also be processed by the applicable provider according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites, government resources, social networks, scheduling tools, product websites, and other external services.

We do not control the privacy practices or content of those websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the type of information involved.

However, no website, device, cloud service, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, ongoing customer relationships, business records, security, dispute resolution, tax or accounting requirements, and other legitimate or legal obligations.

Different types of information may be retained for different periods.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information we maintain about you or to request correction or deletion where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business, security, contractual, accounting, tax, or legal reasons.

You may opt out of nonessential marketing communications at any time.

Business customer data

When Untrained Momentum performs services for another business, that business may control personal information contained in its Microsoft 365 environment, files, customer systems, workflows, or other platforms.

In those situations, Untrained Momentum generally accesses the information only as necessary to perform the requested work and according to the customer's instructions and applicable agreement.

Legal requests and protection

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a valid legal process, protect our rights or property, investigate fraud or security issues, or protect the safety of our customers, business, or others.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as our services, technology, and legal obligations change.

The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
