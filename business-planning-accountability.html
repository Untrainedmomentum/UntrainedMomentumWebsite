How personal support works

We keep coming back to one question: what needs to happen next?

1. Figure out what actually matters

We look at what is happening in the business right now and identify the few things that will make the biggest difference.

2. Turn it into actual work

Instead of “fix website,” we define the next specific action, who owns it, what is blocking it, and when it should happen.

3. Check what happened

If it got done, we move forward. If it did not, we figure out why instead of pretending the plan still works.

4. Improve the systems underneath it

As patterns show up, we improve the website, files, intake, documentation, tools, workflows, or technology creating unnecessary friction.

Before you join

Straight answers.

Who is this for?

Small-business owners and people actively building a business who want clearer priorities, accountability, and practical guidance on the systems behind the work.

Do I have to be starting a new business?

No. Existing business owners are welcome too. In fact, this can be especially useful when a business has grown but the systems and organization never caught up.

Will you tell me exactly what to do?

I will give you recommendations, challenge assumptions, help you evaluate options, and help turn decisions into concrete next steps. You remain the business owner and final decision-maker.

Will you do the work for me?

The planning program itself is focused on guidance, prioritization, and accountability. If you want Untrained Momentum to complete a website, automation, technical setup, or another defined project, we can scope that separately.

What if I only need help with technology or systems?

You may not need an ongoing planning relationship at all. Start with Business Services and we can focus directly on the problem.

Is there a long-term contract?

The planning programs are month-to-month unless a written proposal says otherwise. You can decide each month whether the support is still useful for your business.

Does this guarantee business growth or revenue?

No. No one can reasonably guarantee that. The service gives you a structured planning, accountability, and systems-support process.

What happens after I contact you?

We have a short conversation about your business, what you are trying to accomplish, and where you are getting stuck. If the program makes sense, you will know the price, cadence, responsibilities, and next step before you commit to anything.
