Before you join

Straight answers.

Who is this designed for?

Early-stage and existing small-business owners who are willing to make decisions and complete agreed actions, but want structure, accountability, and practical systems guidance.

Will you make decisions or do the work for me?

You remain the business owner and decision-maker. We help clarify decisions, organize the work, and maintain accountability. Implementation can be scoped separately when you want Untrained Momentum or a specialist to complete defined work.

Does participation guarantee business growth or revenue?

No. Results depend on the offer, market, customer activity, decisions, follow-through, and factors outside any advisor’s control. The service provides a professional planning and accountability process—not a revenue guarantee.

Can I cancel?

The service is month-to-month unless a written proposal states otherwise. Cancellation applies before the next billing period; work already completed and third-party charges remain payable.

What happens after I inquire?

Jen reviews your goals and current stage, then schedules a short fit conversation. If the service is appropriate, you receive the scope, price, meeting cadence, responsibilities, and start date in writing before paying.
