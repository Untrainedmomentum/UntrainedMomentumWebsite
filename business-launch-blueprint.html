A quick reality check

You do not need to look like a giant company to start.

You need a real offer, a way for people to find you, a way for them to contact you, and enough organization that you can actually deliver what you promised.

You can improve the rest as the business grows. The blueprint is meant to help you figure out what matters now—and what can wait.
