Microsoft 365 support FAQ

Questions small businesses usually ask.

Can you help if we already have Microsoft 365?

Yes. Most engagements start with an existing environment that grew over time. The goal is to improve how it is organized and operated without changing things just for the sake of change.

Can you migrate email or files?

Migration work can be evaluated as a defined project. The right approach depends on the current provider, volume of data, business risk, security requirements, and downtime tolerance.

Can you automate Microsoft 365 processes?

Yes. Forms, approvals, notifications, onboarding steps, document routing, and other repetitive work may be good candidates. See our small-business workflow automation service.

Do you provide cybersecurity services?

We can improve administrative practices and coordinate security-related work, but specialized cybersecurity, regulated compliance, and managed monitoring may require a separately scoped security provider.
