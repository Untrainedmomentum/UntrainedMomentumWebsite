Wi-Fi Help FAQ

Quick answers.

Can you make my internet faster?

I can troubleshoot the home-network side, but speed also depends on your internet plan, provider connection, equipment capability, device capability, distance and interference. I will not promise a speed your service cannot deliver.

Do I need a new router?

Not always. We can review the existing setup first. If replacement equipment would likely help, I can explain what features matter before you buy.

Can you help with mesh Wi-Fi?

Yes, I can help set up and configure compatible consumer mesh systems and connect household devices.
