Michigan Resident Agent Service

Looking for a Michigan registered agent?

Michigan generally calls this role a resident agent. You may also see it described or searched for as a registered agent.

A Michigan resident agent is the person or eligible organization designated to receive official documents, notices, demands, and service of process for the business.

The business also has a registered office in Michigan associated with its resident agent.

Untrained Momentum offers Michigan resident-agent service to eligible businesses after reviewing the business, authorized contact, entity information, communication details, and service requirements.

A simple process for an important responsibility.

You remain responsible for maintaining accurate business and contact information and completing required state filings.

Business Startup & Resident Agent FAQs

Straight answers before you start.

Is a resident agent the same as a registered agent in Michigan?

Michigan generally uses the term resident agent. People frequently use the term registered agent when searching for the same type of service. The resident agent is appointed to receive official documents, notices, demands, and service of process for the business.

Can you tell me which entity type I should choose?

I can help you understand general terminology, find official resources, organize your questions, and document the decision you make.

If you need advice about which structure is best for your liability, taxes, ownership arrangement, or specific legal situation, that decision belongs with an attorney and qualified tax professional.

Will you file business forms for me?

Administrative preparation and customer-directed submission support may be available when the filing is straightforward and within the scope of the service.

You review and approve the information. Complex, disputed, regulated, professional, multi-owner, or unusual situations may require a licensed attorney, accountant, tax professional, or another specialist.

Is resident-agent service the same as a mailbox or virtual office?

No. Resident-agent service is for official notices and service of process.

It does not include general business mail, packages, workspace, or permission to represent the registered office as your day-to-day operating business location unless separately agreed in writing.

Are government fees included?

No. Government filing fees, licenses, software subscriptions, domains, hosting, professional services, and other third-party costs are separate unless a written proposal specifically says they are included.

Do I have to be in Michigan to use your startup support?

No. Business startup, technology, website, systems, and planning services are available remotely throughout the United States.

Michigan resident-agent service is specifically for eligible Michigan business entities.

What if I have already started my business?

That is completely fine. A lot of businesses launch first and organize later. We can look at what you already have, identify what is missing or messy, and build a practical cleanup plan.

Do you provide legal, tax, or accounting advice?

No. Untrained Momentum provides administrative, project, operations, and technology support. Legal, tax, and accounting decisions should be handled by appropriately licensed professionals.
