Business-service questions

Straight answers before you call.

Can you set up a POS system for my business?

Yes. I can help configure common POS hardware and software, supported printers and scanners, product catalogs, employee access, testing, and staff orientation using your existing power and network infrastructure. New electrical circuits or other regulated trade work are outside this service.

Do I have to sign a monthly IT contract?

No. One-time troubleshooting, setup, assessments, and defined projects are welcome. Ongoing support can be scoped when it makes sense.

What is the $50 managed website?

The $50/month plan applies to websites built and managed by Untrained Momentum. It includes hosting, routine maintenance, basic technical upkeep, basic SEO setup, and up to two minor content updates per month. A one-time setup fee is quoted based on the size and complexity of the site.

See managed website details →
Can you work on my existing website or another website builder?

Often, yes. Support for websites built or hosted with another provider is separate from the managed website rental. That work is quoted based on the provider, available access, platform limitations, and what you need changed. I prefer building and managing my own sites because it gives me more control over quality and maintenance, but I can evaluate an existing setup before recommending a path forward.

Can you help a brand-new business set everything up?

Yes. Support can include domains, professional email, websites, business listings, POS setup, computers, printers, forms, booking, and a practical launch checklist.

Do you work outside Big Rapids?

Yes. On-site small-business support is available in Mid-Michigan depending on project location and scope. Many website, Microsoft 365, systems, automation, and project services are available remotely across the United States.
