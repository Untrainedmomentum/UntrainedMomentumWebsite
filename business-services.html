Good to Know

Flexible help without making everything a contract.

Do I have to sign a monthly IT contract?

No. One-time troubleshooting, setup, assessments, and defined projects are welcome. Ongoing support can be scoped when it makes sense.

Can you help with a website built somewhere else?

Often, yes. Existing provider and website-builder work is quoted separately based on the platform, access, limitations, and scope.

Can you help a brand-new business?

Yes. I can help coordinate the website, domain, email, POS, forms, booking, devices, accounts, and other technology needed to get started.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Many website, Microsoft 365, automation, systems, and project services can be handled remotely across the United States. On-site support is available in Mid-Michigan depending on location and scope.
