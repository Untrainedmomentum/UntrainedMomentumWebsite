Business-service questions

Straight answers before we start.

Do you provide traditional 24/7 managed IT services?

No. Untrained Momentum provides practical support, projects, administration, documentation, and operational improvement. If your business requires a 24/7 help desk, security operations center, or around-the-clock monitoring, we can help define the need and coordinate an appropriate specialist.

Can you work with our existing IT provider or vendors?

Yes. Vendor coordination and translating between operational and technical stakeholders are core strengths. The goal is clearer ownership and better outcomes, not unnecessary replacement.

Do you work outside Michigan?

Yes. Business services are available remotely across the United States. On-site availability depends on location and project scope.

Can you automate a process that is currently manual?

Often, yes. We begin by understanding and simplifying the process, risks, exceptions, and ownership. Automation comes after the workflow is clear.

Do you support both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Microsoft 365, Power Automate, documentation, and business process work are central strengths. Other tools can be supported when they fit the engagement, with specialist coordination when needed.

What is included in the $50-per-month website service?

Plans start at $50 per month and include hosting, routine maintenance, technical updates, and an ongoing point of contact. Website size, custom features, content creation, ecommerce, and the amount of recurring editing can affect the final price. Your scope and any setup cost are agreed before work begins.

Is the monthly planning service the same as unlimited IT support?

No. The monthly service provides planning, accountability, and guidance about the technology and systems behind your business. Hands-on implementation, troubleshooting, development, and third-party costs are scoped and quoted separately so expectations remain clear.
