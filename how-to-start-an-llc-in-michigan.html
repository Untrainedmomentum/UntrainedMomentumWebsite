Michigan business startup guide · Updated 2026

How to start an LLC in Michigan without losing the business behind the paperwork.

Forming the LLC is only one part of launching a business. This guide separates the official Michigan filing steps from the practical work of setting up banking, technology, email, a website, and repeatable operations.

Official formation step

1. File Articles of Organization with Michigan LARA.

Michigan's Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs says a Michigan LLC is formed by completing and submitting Articles of Organization to the Corporations Division with the applicable filing fee. LARA's online filing process uses MiLogin for Business and the domestic LLC Articles of Organization form.

Before filing, make sure the proposed name, ownership plan, business purpose, contact information, and resident-agent details are organized. Filing creates the legal entity; it does not automatically create every license, tax registration, bank account, or operating system the business may need.

Resident agent and registered office

2. Designate a Michigan resident agent and registered office.

Michigan requires the Articles of Organization to include a resident agent and registered office.

The resident agent is designated to receive official documents, notices, demands, and service of process for the company. The registered office is the resident agent's physical location and may be different from the company's main business location.

After formation

3. Build the operating foundation.

01

EIN and tax setup

Determine what federal and state registrations apply. An EIN is commonly needed for banking, payroll, and other business functions. Tax questions should be reviewed with a qualified tax professional.

02

Business bank account

Keep business and personal finances separated and ask the bank what formation documents and identification it requires.

03

Licenses and permits

LLC formation does not automatically authorize every type of business activity. Check state, local, professional, and industry-specific requirements that apply to what you do.

04

Domain and professional email

Secure the domain and create business-controlled email accounts. Avoid building critical accounts around a personal email address that cannot be cleanly handed off later.

05

Website and Google presence

Build a clear website, contact path, and accurate business listings so customers can understand the offer and know how to reach you.

06

Basic operating systems

Document how leads, estimates, customer communication, files, invoices, onboarding, passwords, and recurring responsibilities will be handled.

A practical launch order

4. Do the next necessary thing—not every possible thing.

Confirm the business model

Know what you sell, who buys it, how you will charge, and what you need in order to deliver it.

Handle the legal and administrative foundation

Complete the entity, resident-agent, registration, licensing, tax, insurance, and banking work that applies to the business.

Build the customer path

Create the domain, email, website, phone/contact process, and a simple way for leads to become paying customers.

Document the repeatable work

Once the process works, write it down and automate only the steps that are truly repetitive.

Michigan LLC FAQ

Common questions.

Does Michigan call it a registered agent or resident agent?

Michigan uses the term resident agent. Other states often use the term registered agent for a similar role.

Does forming an LLC give me a business license?

No. Michigan LARA explains that the Corporations Division receives and files business-entity documents but does not license the business to perform specific services. Separate licenses or approvals may apply depending on the activity.
