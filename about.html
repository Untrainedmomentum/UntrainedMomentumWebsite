How I work

Expertise is most valuable when it makes things clearer.

Listen before diagnosing

The first version of a technical or operational problem is rarely the whole problem. I start with what you are trying to accomplish and what is getting in the way.

Use plain language

You should understand your options well enough to make a confident decision. Jargon is never a substitute for clarity.

Choose the smallest sensible solution

More software, complexity, and recurring cost are not automatically better. The solution should fit the person, team, budget, risk, and actual business need.

Leave the work better than I found it

That may mean documentation, cleanup, training, safer practices, clearer ownership, or a plan for what happens next—not just a temporary fix.
