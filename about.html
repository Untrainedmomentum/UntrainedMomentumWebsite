About Untrained Momentum

Serious experience. Straightforward help.

How I work

Expertise is most valuable when it makes things clearer.

Listen before diagnosing

The first version of a technical problem is rarely the whole problem. I start with what you are trying to accomplish and what is getting in the way.

Use plain language

You should understand your options well enough to make a confident decision. Jargon is never a substitute for clarity.

Choose the smallest sensible solution

More software, complexity, and recurring cost are not automatically better. The solution should fit the person, team, budget, and risk.

Leave the work better than I found it

That may mean documentation, cleanup, training, safer practices, or a plan for what happens next—not just a temporary fix.

What the name means

Momentum is not about moving fast. It is about removing what keeps you stuck.

“Untrained” is a reminder that the most useful ideas do not always arrive through the expected path. Curiosity, hard-earned experience, and the willingness to question a broken process can create better outcomes than following convention for its own sake.

If technology is getting in the way, let’s make it useful again.
