Senior Tech Help FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to know what is wrong before I call?

No. Describe what you are trying to do and what happens when you try. That is enough to start.

Can you help organize passwords?

I can help you improve password practices, recovery information and multi-factor authentication, and choose a password-management approach that fits your comfort level. You should keep control of your own permanent passwords.

Can you help identify suspicious messages or pop-ups?

I can help review common signs of scams or suspicious technology behavior and help secure accounts or devices when appropriate. If money was sent or sensitive financial information was exposed, contacting the relevant bank, card issuer, platform, or law enforcement may also be necessary.
