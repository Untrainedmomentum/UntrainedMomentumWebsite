What Counts as an Update?

Small changes should stay small.

Your two included monthly updates are intended for routine content changes, not redesigns or new functionality.

Examples include changing business hours, replacing a photo, updating a phone number, changing a price, adjusting existing wording, adding a short announcement, or updating a staff bio.

Unused updates do not roll over. Larger changes, new pages, redesign work, integrations, CRM features, databases, or new functionality are quoted separately.

A Managed Service

What “website rental” means.

You are paying for an active managed website service rather than purchasing the website build outright.

Your business keeps ownership of its domain, logo, original photos, business information, and customer data. The website design, build, and managed website service remain with Untrained Momentum while your subscription is active.

If the managed website subscription ends, the hosted managed website service ends as well. This structure is what allows us to keep the initial setup cost and monthly price accessible for small businesses.
